REMEMBER

Five habits that stop most rushed decisions.

  • Pause when a message creates urgency, fear, or pressure.
  • Verify independently — never through the suspicious message itself.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or recovery codes.
  • Inspect links, payment requests, QR codes and attachments carefully.
  • If money is lost, report immediately and preserve evidence.

FIRST 10 MINUTES

Do these things first

01

Stop the interaction

Do not send more money, codes, passwords or personal information. End suspicious calls/chats.

02

Call 1930 if money was lost

For financial cyber fraud, report immediately through the national helpline.

03

Inform your bank / payment provider

Use the official app, website or contact details — not the scammer's message.

04

Secure affected accounts

Change compromised passwords and review account sessions, cards and payment settings where relevant.

05

Preserve evidence

Keep transaction IDs, screenshots, phone numbers, email addresses, URLs, dates and times.
